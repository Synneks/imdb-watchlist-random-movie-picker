How to export your IMDb watchlist CSV

IMDb does not let websites read your watchlist directly. Export a CSV from the desktop site, then upload it here. Takes about a minute.

  1. Open your IMDb watchlist on a computer (export is not available in the IMDb app).
  2. Log in if you are not already signed in.
  3. Open the three-dot menu (⋯) in the top-right of the list, then choose Export.
  4. Save the downloaded .csv file (often named something like WATCHLIST.csv).
  5. Come back here and click Import watchlist to upload that file.

Your file stays on this device in local storage. Nothing is uploaded to a server.

FAQ

Is this an official IMDb tool?

No. It is a free helper that uses IMDb’s own CSV export. Titles open back on IMDb.

Does it work with custom lists?

Yes. Export any IMDb list the same way (⋯ → Export) and upload that CSV.

Why is there no poster or plot?

The official CSV includes title, year, rating, runtime, and directors — not posters, plot, Metascore, or cast. Those stay on IMDb.
