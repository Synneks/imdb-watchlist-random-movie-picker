How to import a list

How to export an IMDb watchlist or custom list CSV

IMDb does not let websites read your watchlist directly. Export a CSV from the desktop site, then upload it here. Takes about a minute.

  1. Open your IMDb watchlist or a custom list on a computer (export is not available in the IMDb app).
  2. Log in if you are not already signed in.
  3. Open the three-dot menu (⋯) in the top-right of the list, then choose Export.
  4. Save the downloaded .csv file (often named something like WATCHLIST.csv).
  5. Come back here and click Import IMDb CSV to upload that file.

Your file stays on this device in local storage. Nothing is uploaded to a server.

Import a Letterboxd watchlist or custom list CSV

  1. Request your data export from Letterboxd account settings.
  2. Download and extract the ZIP on your device. Choose watchlist.csv or an individual custom-list CSV.
  3. With Letterboxd selected above, import the extracted .csv file. ZIP uploads and pasted list URLs are not supported.

Use an exported file with film names, years, and Letterboxd film links. Each provider keeps its own saved list and filter here; switching providers never combines them.

Custom-list exports

In Letterboxd Settings on desktop, export your account data and extract the ZIP. Choose the CSV for your custom list from the lists folder, then click Import Letterboxd CSV. Upload the extracted CSV, including its list metadata and film table. The list name is saved when present; otherwise the filename is shown. ZIP upload and pasted list URLs are not supported.

Frequently asked questions

Is this an official tool?

No. Random Movie Picker is an independent helper, unaffiliated with IMDb or Letterboxd. Film links open on their original provider.

Does it work with custom lists?

Yes. Import an IMDb list export or an extracted Letterboxd custom-list CSV, with the matching provider selected.

Why is plot / Metascore missing?

The official CSV includes title, year, rating, runtime, and directors. Posters and top billed stars are loaded when you pick a title. Plot and Metascore are not in the export — open the title on IMDb for the full page.

Why are details missing?

Letterboxd CSVs can contain only a name, year, and film link. Picks work with those fields; missing posters, ratings, and credits stay empty. Letterboxd-only picks do not request IMDb metadata.

Not affiliated with IMDb, Amazon, or Letterboxd. CSV files are read locally and lists are saved in this browser. IMDb picks can request posters and cast from IMDb; fonts and images may load from external hosts.
